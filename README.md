# Meal Recipe App

Meal Recipe App, This app provides a collection of delicious meal recipes, including detailed instructions, ingredients, and tips. You can explore recipes, search for specific meals, or discover a random recipe each time you visit. 

## Features

- **Random Recipe**: Click the "Get Another Random Meal" button to get a new recipe each time.
- **Search for Meals**: Search for meals based on ingredients or type (e.g., chicken).
- **Recipe Details**: View a detailed recipe with ingredients, instructions, and preparation steps.
- **Meal Filters**: Filter recipes by specific categories like chicken, beef, vegetarian, etc.

## Example Recipe: Lamb, Tomato, and Sweet Spices

### Ingredients:
- Pickled vine leaves preserved in brine (small delicate leaves are better)
- Basmati rice with butter and pine nuts (ideal accompaniment)
- Couscous (optional)

### Instructions:
1. **Prepare the Filling**:
   - In a bowl, combine all ingredients except tomatoes. Cut tomatoes in half, coarsely grate them, and discard skins. Add salt and pepper, then stir. Let it sit for up to a day in the fridge.
   - Before using, squeeze out excess juice.

2. **Make the Sauce**:
   - Heat oil in a medium pan. Add ginger and garlic, cooking for 1-2 minutes (do not burn). Add tomatoes, lemon juice, and sugar. Season and simmer for 20 minutes.

3. **Prepare the Vine Leaves**:
   - Line a wide pan with any torn or broken leaves, then add fennel slices.
   - Place a prepared vine leaf (veiny side up), add filling, fold the sides, and roll tightly.
   - Repeat with remaining leaves, arranging tightly in the pan.

4. **Cook**:
   - Pour the sauce over the leaves (add water if needed to cover). Place a plate on top, cover, and bring to a boil.
   - Reduce heat and simmer for 70 minutes, until most of the liquid evaporates.
   - Serve warm with rice, garnished with coriander.

## How to Use

1. **View Random Recipe**: Click the "Get Another Random Meal" button to display a random recipe each time.
2. **Search Meals**: Use the search bar to find meals based on ingredients or category.
3. **Meal Filters**: Filter recipes by ingredient types such as chicken, vegetarian, etc.
